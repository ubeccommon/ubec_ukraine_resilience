Manual / interactive sources (open the page, use its export or request access):

Bellingcat Civilian Harm in Ukraine TimeMap
  https://ukraine.bellingcat.com/           (map; "Download data" link exports CSV)
Eyes on Russia / Russia-Ukraine Monitor Map (Centre for Information Resilience)
  https://eyesonrussia.org/                 (map + database; export via map, or request)
GeoConfirmed
  https://geoconfirmed.org/                 (geolocated footage; GeoJSON/KML export in app)
Texty.org.ua attack map
  https://texty.org.ua/                     (search "карта ракетних ударів")
DiXi Group energy-infrastructure strike log (2022-09 to 2024-08, CSV)
  https://map.ua-energy.org/en/resources/12f3148d-841a-478d-b9ed-72bf0764b286
UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset (annual, academic)
  https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/
ISW / CTP daily frontline shapefiles (ArcGIS Hub)
  https://www.understandingwar.org/backgrounder/interactive-map-russias-invasion-ukraine
DeepStateMap (frontline, subscription for exports)
  https://deepstatemap.live/
UNOSAT damage assessments (city-level building damage layers)
  https://unosat.org/products
Copernicus Emergency Management Service activations for Ukraine
  https://emergency.copernicus.eu/
KSE Institute "Russia Will Pay" damage reports (aggregate, PDF/XLSX)
  https://kse.ua/russia-will-pay/
CSIS Russian Firepower Strike Tracker (dashboard built on the Kaggle missile data)
  https://www.csis.org/programs/futures-lab/projects/russian-firepower-strike-tracker-analyzing-missile-attacks-ukraine
